# Aadhi Code

Website for **Aadhi Code**, a technology company based in Baluwatar, Kathmandu.

## About Aadhi Code

Aadhi Code helps organizations turn ideas into reliable digital products through thoughtful design, modern technology, and collaborative development.

## What You'll Find on Our Website

- **About Us** – Learn about Aadhi Code, our values, and our goals.
- **Members** – Meet the people behind our work and discover our team’s expertise.
- **Previous Works** – Explore selected projects and solutions we have delivered.
- **Projects** – View our current and upcoming initiatives.
- **Manpower** – Learn about our capabilities, roles, and technical talent.
- **Collaborations** – Discover opportunities to work with Aadhi Code.
- **Contact** – Get in touch with our team.

## Our Goals

- Build useful, accessible, and dependable technology.
- Deliver high-quality solutions that create lasting value.
- Support innovation through teamwork and collaboration.
- Grow a skilled and motivated technology community.

## Location

**Baluwatar, Kathmandu, Nepal**
